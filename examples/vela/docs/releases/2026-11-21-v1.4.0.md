## 6. Runbook (embedded — short)

- Backlog alert → check worker alive (`worker_ps`), Mailhaus status page; restart worker.
- Parked rows → inspect `email_outbox WHERE status='parked'`, fix cause, replay via `scripts/replay-outbox` (idempotent by outbox row id).
- Flag off → `vela flags set order_emails off` (instant, no deploy).

## 7. Rollback trigger criteria

- 5xx on /me/notification-preferences >2× baseline for 5 min → flag off.
- outbox_parked_total >0 for 10 min → stop worker, investigate before resume.
- checkout completion <60% for 15 min → flag off (independent of cause).
