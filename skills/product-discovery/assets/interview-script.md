# Interview script: <problem area>

Target: <persona>, recruited via <source>. Duration: 30 min. Record with permission.

## 1. Warm-up (2 min)

- "Tell me about your role and what a normal day looks like."
- "What tools do you spend most of your day in?"

## 2. Context (5 min)

- "Walk me through the last time you dealt with <problem area>. Start from the beginning."

## 3. Deep dive (15 min) — Mom Test rules: past behavior only, no pitching

- "What did you do right before that? What came right after?"
- "What was the hardest part? Why?"
- "How often does that happen? When did it last happen?"
- "What did it cost you — time, money, or something else?"
- "What have you tried to fix it? What happened with that?"
- "Who else gets pulled in when this breaks?"

## 4. Current tools (5 min)

- "Show me how you handle this today." (screen-share beats description)
- "What do you like about that setup? What annoys you?"

## 5. Close (3 min)

- "Anything I should have asked about this but didn't?"
- "Who else deals with this and would talk to me?"

## Rules

- Do NOT mention the idea or any solution.
- Ask "why" until you hit motivation, then stop.
- Write the 3 most surprising things down within an hour — memory rewrites itself.
